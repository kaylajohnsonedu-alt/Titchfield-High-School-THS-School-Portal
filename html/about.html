About Us

School History

Titchfield High School is a historic high school located at 22 Fort George Street in Port Antonio, Portland, Jamaica. Founded in January 1786, it stands as the fifth-oldest high school in Jamaica.

• Foundation (1786): Originally established as a "free school" under the Titchfield Trust to provide education in reading, writing, arithmetic, Greek, and Latin (primarily for poor white children of the era, as no formal system existed for enslaved children).

• Fort George Relocation (1883–1886): The Jamaica School Commission took over management from the trust in 1883 and later relocated the school into the historic Fort George military barracks—a site originally built in 1729 featuring 10-foot walls and cannons overlooking the bay.

• The Major Plant Era (1885): Headmaster Major W.H. Plant shaped and transformed the institution into a prominent modern school.

• Transition to Modern THS (1927): The school completed its transition into the unified Titchfield High School operating across grades 7 through 13.
