About Us

School History

Titchfield High School is a historic high school located at 22 Fort George Street in Port Antonio, Portland, Jamaica. Founded in January 1786, it stands as the fifth-oldest high school in Jamaica.

• Foundation (1786): Originally established as a "free school" under the Titchfield Trust to provide education in reading, writing, arithmetic, Greek, and Latin (primarily for poor white children of the era, as no formal system existed for enslaved children).

• Fort George Relocation (1883–1886): The Jamaica School Commission took over management from the trust in 1883 and later relocated the school into the historic Fort George military barracks—a site originally built in 1729 featuring 10-foot walls and cannons overlooking the bay.

• The Major Plant Era (1885): Headmaster Major W.H. Plant shaped and transformed the institution into a prominent modern school.

• Transition to Modern THS (1927): The school completed its transition into the unified Titchfield High School operating across grades 7 through 13.

Landmark Accomplishments

Titchfield High School's landmark accomplishments span over two centuries of pioneering education, national quiz championships, and historic athletic firsts in Jamaica.

Academic & Institutional Milestones
• Fifth-Oldest High School in Jamaica (1786). Established in January 1786 under the Titchfield Trust, it is one of the Caribbean's oldest surviving secondary institutions.

• National Heritage Site Campus (1886). Relocated into the historic 18th-century Fort George military barracks in Port Antonio, operating on grounds protected by the Jamaica National Heritage Trust.

• 2016 TVJ Schools' Challenge Quiz Champions. Defeated Campion College to become the first rural school in nearly 20 years—and only the 13th school ever—to win Jamaica's premier high school quiz competition.

• 2025 TVJ Schools' Challenge Quiz Champions. Captured their second national title by defeating Jamaica College 26–23 in the championship match.

• National Academic Honors. Consistently maintains strong regional exam results (averaging 85% CSEC and 88% CAPE pass rates), producing top national CAPE students in Applied Mathematics and Electrical & Electronic Engineering Technology, as well as earning the Ministry of Education's Spirit of Care Award for overall academic and athletic excellence.


Athletic Milestones
• First Back-to-Back Girls' Champs Winners (1963–1964). Made history as the first school to win consecutive Inter-Secondary Schools Girls' Championships titles.

• Founding Member of the daCosta Cup (1950). Competed as one of the four original schools in the inaugural rural schoolboy football season and went on to reach three daCosta Cup finals (1964, 1982, and 2003).

• Eastern Champs Dominance. Captured the 2025 Eastern Athletics Championships and successfully defended the Eastern Champs boys' title in 2026, alongside winning national U-19 boys' table tennis titles.
